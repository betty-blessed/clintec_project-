Ultrasound Services

Accurate and reliable ultrasound imaging.

Early Pregnancy Scan

Usually done in 1st trimester to help confirm pregnancy, check the baby heartbeat and growth, estimate gestational age, determine location of the pregnancy. Also help rule out ectopic pregnancy, miscarriage and bleeding complications.

Book Scan

Obstetrics_Pregnancy Scan

Anomaly scan usually done in 2nd or 3rd trimester is used to monitor your baby growth, development and wellbeing. It checks the baby position, heartbeat, movements, placenta, amniotic fluid and overall growth, while helping detect/rule out any anomalies and pregnancy complications.

Pelvic Ultrasound_Scan

It checks the uterus, ovaries, cervix and bladder. Helps detect/rule out cysts, fibroids, pelvic infections, abnormal bleeding, infertility issues, tumors, pregnancy-related concerns,ectopic pregnancy and urinary problems.

Abdomen Ultrasound_Scan

It views organs in the abdomen such as the liver, gallbladder, kidneys, spleen and pancreas. It helps assess pain, infection, stones, swelling, fluid collection, masses, and other abdominal organ conditions, may help rule out gallstones, kidney stones, liver disease, and fluid abnormalities.
